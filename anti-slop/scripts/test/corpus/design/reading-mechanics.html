The night ferry

The last boat leaves the harbour at eleven and reaches the island a little after midnight. Most passengers sleep on the benches below deck, while a few stand at the rail and watch the lights of the town grow small, then disappear behind the headland as the wind picks up.

Tickets bought on board cost two pounds more than tickets bought at the kiosk, which opens an hour before each sailing and closes when the gangway is raised.

Bicycles travel free, but they must be lashed to the rail on the car deck before departure.
